# Paladex promo — script v1

**Length:** 30 seconds · **Format:** 1080×1920 vertical (Reels/TikTok/Shorts), with a 1920×1080 cut later
**Voice:** calm, confident, one person talking to a player, not an announcer
**Music:** low, minimal electronic beat; drops out for the last line

Everything shown must be the real site. No fake reviews, ratings, prices or "official" claims
(see PRODUCT.md honesty rules). Don't show Compare; it's removed from the site.

---

## English (site UI is English, so this is the main version)

| Time | On screen | Voiceover | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Black screen. A racket fades in on the black studio and starts turning. | *You're about to spend 200 euros on a racket…* | |
| 3–6s | Quick cuts of generic racket thumbnails / a shop grid feel (blurred, no real brand pages). | *…based on a photo and a spec sheet you don't understand.* | Round? Teardrop? Diamond? |
| 6–12s | Paladex racket page: drag to rotate the 3D model, zoom on the face. | *On Paladex, you turn every racket in 3D,* | Every racket in 3D |
| 12–17s | Specs panel: balance / shape / thickness with their plain-language explanations. | *and every spec tells you what it means for your game.* | Specs in plain words |
| 17–23s | "Find my racket" quiz: tap through 2–3 answers, then the "Where do you win your points?" court, then the 3D matches appear. | *Answer a few questions, and see the rackets that fit how you actually play.* | Find my racket |
| 23–27s | Brand rail on /rackets, tap a brand, rackets filter in place. | *Every brand, one place.* | |
| 27–30s | Logo mark on black. URL below. Music cuts. | *Paladex. Choose your next racket in 3D.* | paladex · free, no sign-up |

**Word count:** ~60 words → fits 30s with breathing room.

## Things to check before publishing

      including placeholders. Placeholder models are labelled on the site; the ad shouldn't
      zoom on that label or hide it misleadingly.
- [ ] "Paladex" trademark is not checked yet (PRODUCT.md). Check before paying for ads.
- [ ] The final URL/domain to show at the end.
- [ ] If any affiliate link appears on screen, it must be real (not `AFFILIATE_URL_HERE`).
