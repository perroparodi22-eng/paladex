# Rackets without their real 3D design

**Status 8 Oct 2026: 13 of the 14 are fixed.** 61 of the 62 rackets now have a 3D model traced from real photos. The only one left is the **Black Crown Piton 5.0**: it is discontinued (it looks like a 2021 model, not 2025), and no shop still shows a photo of it.

## Fixed on 8 Oct 2026

For each racket we:
1. Downloaded straight-on photos from a shop listing.
2. Cut out the photo, traced the 3D outline, built the model and checked it in 3D.
3. Published the photos (front plus 2–3 other views) to the racket page.
4. Corrected specs where the brand listing disagreed with the old sample data.

## Known limits of the new models

- The **Wilson Bela Pro V3** and **Babolat Technical Viper Soft 3.0** photos are slightly off-axis. Their outlines were rebuilt from the cleaner half of the photo, so the shape is symmetric, but the print still shows a little of the frame's side on one edge.
- The **Babolat Viper Soft** throat slots are white on a white background, so they were marked by hand from the photo (`--cut` in the cut-out tool).
- **HEAD rackets** with white backgrounds now have see-through holes, unlike the older HEAD models.
- Photo licences are still pending, as for the rest of the catalogue.
